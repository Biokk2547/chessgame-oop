===================================================================
                     CHESS GAME OOP (LibGDX)
===================================================================

HOW TO PLAY:
1. Double-click "Play.bat" or "Play_Silent.vbs" to launch the game!
   (You can also double-click "ChessGame.jar" directly)
2. To play in console text mode, double-click "Play_Console_Mode.bat".

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- Java 11 or higher installed (Java 17 / 21 LTS recommended)
  Free download at: https://adoptium.net/

FEATURES:
- Full GUI powered by LibGDX LWJGL3 with high-resolution piece graphics
- AI opponents with custom styles: Aggressive, Defensive, Positional, Tactical, Master
- Integrated Stockfish 18 UCI engine
- 4 Epic Boss Battles with unique mechanics and dialogue
- 100+ Chess Tactical Puzzles
- Complete Game Review & Analysis screen with accuracy score & blunder detection
- Move history, FEN/PGN export, Undo/Redo, and dynamic evaluation bar

Enjoy the game!
